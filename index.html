01 / ABOUT

Understanding nature, one structure at a time.

QFT∞

I'm a theoretical physics researcher drawn to the mathematical structures underlying interacting quantum systems. A recurring question in my work is how complicated microscopic theories can reveal simple and sometimes unexpected behavior at long distances.

My interests span large-N field theories, fixed points, supersymmetry, three-dimensional gauge–matter systems, and the tools of perturbative and Wilsonian renormalization. I enjoy moving between concrete calculations and the bigger conceptual picture.
